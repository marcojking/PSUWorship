# How We Lead

Worship Music and Arts Club at Penn State — the things we hold each other to.

These aren't bylaws. They're the handful of things that, if we get them wrong,
make the whole thing not worth doing. Anyone on leadership can call anyone else
back to these, including Marco.

---

## 1. If you get prideful about your position, say it out loud

If you ever notice yourself getting prideful or boastful about being in
leadership here — for any reason — go talk about it with another person on the
team. Not as a confession ritual. Just get it into the light before it grows.

**Why:** Christian bubble popularity games are the poison that kills groups like
this. The moment a leadership title becomes something to *have* rather than
something to *do*, we've already lost the thing we were building. This is all
about serving others. A position here is a job, not a rank.

## 2. The goal is to reach the lost

That's the point. Everything else is downstream of it.

This doesn't mean we can't make events or music for Christians — we will, and
that's good. But we don't get to use that as a crutch. If everything we make
only ever lands on people who are already in, we've quietly stopped doing the
thing we said we'd do.

## 3. Our art should be excellent

Not "good for a college club." Not "good for church music." Excellent.

Excellence is part of the witness. Sloppy work tells people this doesn't
actually matter to us, and they're right to believe us when we tell them that.
We've got real resources — use them.

---

*Add to this as we go. If a rule stops being true, we take it out rather than
letting it sit here being ignored.*
